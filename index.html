Chess Tactics — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Chess Tactics is an offline chess puzzle app. This page describes what the app does and does not do with your data.

Data we collect

None. Chess Tactics has no user accounts, no server, and no analytics. Your puzzle progress, local rating, and app settings are stored only on your device (locally, via standard on-device storage) and are never transmitted anywhere. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Advertising

Unless you have purchased the ad-removal option, the app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to select and measure ads, under Google's own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. We do not receive or store any of this data ourselves.

Purchases

The one-time ad-removal purchase is processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Puzzle content

Chess tactics puzzles are sourced from the Lichess puzzle database, released under a CC0 (public domain) license.

Children's privacy

Chess Tactics does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL with a revised "last updated" date.

Contact
